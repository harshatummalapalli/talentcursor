The Method

The TalentCursor Method

A battle-tested 15-day sprint process for predictable hiring outcomes.

1

Deep Discovery

We map your tech stack, culture, growth trajectory, and ideal candidate DNA. Not just requirements — strategy.

2

Precision Sourcing

Multi-channel outreach combining LinkedIn, GitHub, communities, and our proprietary network. Every pipeline is curated, not spray-and-prayed.

Rigorous Vetting
